# Eigenstate

An independent project focused on building a quantitative trading system that uses options positioning and market data to identify viable trading setups across selected futures markets. 

## Current approach

### Eigenstate Terminal:
- Displays market data, options positioning, strike profiles and high-probability algorithmically generated price levels
- Visualises dealer positioning, hedging pressure, volatility levels and other relevant market context
- Identifies confluence between levels and evaluates their significance under current market conditions
- Classifies market regimes and ranks potential trading opportunities

### Eigenstate Trading Bot:
- Processes market and options data to identify potential trading setups
- Evaluates level significance, market context and predefined entry conditions
- Generates trading signals and executes trades according to defined rules
- Manages position sizing, stop-losses, profit targets and risk limits
- Records level interactions, trading decisions and outcomes for performance analysis
